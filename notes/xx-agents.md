# Getting Free Coding Agent Subscriptions

As a student, you have access to several powerful AI coding assistants for free.

## Claude Code

Make a post in Ed Discussion and ask providing your bu.edu email (required for the BU Team Plan). First come first served :)

**Quickstart:** [code.claude.com/docs/en/quickstart](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/quickstart)

## Google Gemini

Google offers models for students (Gemini 3 Pro as well as others) through the Gemini Pro plan.

1.  **Sign Up**:
    *   Go to [gemini.google/students](https://gemini.google/students/).
    *   Eligible university students can get one free year of the Gemini Pro plan.

2.  **Antigravity**:
    *   Antigravity is Google's agentic AI coding environment. You can download it at [antigravity.google](https://antigravity.google/) and use your Gemini Pro plan with it.

## Cursor

Cursor is an AI-powered code editor (forked from VS Code).

1.  **Student Plan**:
    *   Eligible university students get **one free year of Cursor Pro**.
    *   Go to [cursor.com/students](https://cursor.com/students) to sign up.
2.  **Download**:
    *   Download the editor from [cursor.com](https://cursor.com).
